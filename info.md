- PPM started as a private initiative in December 2019 and merged with

  young and dedicated individuals
- Registered with SEC as a non-stock and non-
  profit organization with SEC Reg. No. 2021080021580-27
- Relies on volunteers, donations, and fundraising activities
- PPM is not a shelter but actively cooperates with rescue and animal welfare groups

# Community Cat Management Program

- The Community Cat Management Program is a
  Trap-Neuter-Return initiative designed to
  effectively control and manage feral and
  free-roaming cat population. TNR stops the
  breeding cycle of cats and therefore improves their
  lives while preventing reproduction.
- The first project started in Cebu Business Park as early
  as 2016 through an individual initiative by SvetRescue.
  The Community Cat Management Program in Cebu
  Business Park is the first TNVR model community in
  Cebu.
- In 2020, the Cats of Andres Guadalupe Project started
  its initiative in the middle of a pandemic lockdown.
- All cats are spayed/neutered, vaccinated against rabies,
  and identified with earnotch.
- Our dedicated volunteers feed over 100 community cats on a daily basis in both managed colonies.

# Trap-Neuter-Return

or Trap-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return (TNVR) is the least
expensive, most effective, and most compassionate
method of controlling stray cat populations. The
following are the basic steps to conducting TNVR:

- TRAP - Humanely trap all the cats in a
  colony.
- NEUTER (or Spay) - Take the cats in their
  traps to a veterinarian or clinic to be
  neutered, vaccinated, and ear-tipped
- VACCINATE - Vaccinate with anti-rabies
- RETURN - After the cats recover, return
  them to their outdoor home where they
  were trapped.

A colony caretaker, who supplies food
and conducts health monitoring, is also
a part of TNR.

TNVR addresses very important issues: (1) overpopulation and (2) rabies.
It is a method used to CONTROL the size of the street cat population by capturing the cats,
spaying/neutering them, and then returning them back to where they came from.

# Community Pawntry Drive

- The Community Pawntry Drive is a donation
  initiative aimed at supporting local animal shelters,
  rescue organizations, and individuals in the animal
  welfare community.
- This drive was inspired by the community pantry
  movement that emerged across the country during the
  pandemic lockdown.
- Since its inception in December 2019, the Community
  Pawntry Drive has evolved into a regular event for Paw
  Pantry Mission. Over the past five years, the initiative has:
  - Received cash donations over ₱518,000 to fund pet food,
    supplies, and logistical needs
  - Distributed over 5,000 kilograms of pet food to deserving
    beneficiaries, chosen by the public
  - Supported an average of 15-30 beneficiaries per drive, with
    the number varying on the resources available for each event
