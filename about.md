<section class="container">
  <h2 class="section-title">Our Story</h2>
  <div style="max-width: 800px; margin: 0 auto; text-align: left;">
    <p style="font-size: 1.1rem; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
      Paw Pantry Mission was founded on the simple belief that pets are family members who deserve love, care, and proper nutrition regardless of their family's financial circumstances. We understand that life can present unexpected challenges, and sometimes families face difficult choices about caring for their beloved companions.
    </p>
    <p style="font-size: 1.1rem; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
      Our organization emerged from the recognition that many pet surrenders to shelters happen not because families don't love their pets, but because they temporarily cannot afford to feed them. We believe that with the right support and resources, these families can overcome their challenges while keeping their pets at home where they belong.
    </p>
    <p style="font-size: 1.1rem; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
      Through community partnerships, volunteer dedication, and the generosity of donors, we've been able to help numerous families during their times of need, ensuring that financial hardship never becomes a reason to surrender a beloved pet.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
