# About Paw Pantry Mission

Paw Pantry Mission Inc. is a SEC-registered nonprofit organization dedicated to promoting the responsible feeding and sterilization of stray animals. We advocate for the Community Cat Management Program, a Trap-Neuter-Return initiative, designed to effectively manage the stray animal population while enhancing their quality of life. Our goal is to build communities where people value coexistence with animals and treat them with respect and kindness.

## Our History

**2019** - Started as a private initiative in December with our first Community Pawntry Drive, merging with SvetRescue project that had a trustworthy reputation since 2016 in the animal welfare field.

**2021** - Officially registered with SEC as non-stock and nonprofit organization (SEC Reg. No. 2021080021580-27).

**2022** - Partnered with Cebu Business Park for the Community Cat Management Program, a TNR initiative.

## Key Programs

### Trap-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return (TNVR)
The most effective and compassionate method of controlling stray cat populations. We humanely trap cats, have them spayed/neutered and vaccinated against rabies, then return them to their communities with dedicated caretakers who provide ongoing food and health monitoring.

**Impact**: Over 114 community cats spayed and neutered, preventing over 2,000 unwanted kittens from being born on the streets.

### Community Pawntry Drive
A bi-annual donation initiative supporting local animal shelters, rescue organizations, and individuals in the animal welfare community. Inspired by the community pantry movement during the pandemic.

**Impact**: Over ₱518,000 in cash donations raised and 5,000+ kilograms of pet food distributed to 15-30 beneficiaries per drive.

### Community Cat Feeding Program
Daily feeding and monitoring of 100+ community cats in our managed colonies at Cebu Business Park and Andres Guadalupe.

### Monthly Food Drive for Cebu City Pound
Regular assistance for the resident cats and dogs of Cebu City Animal Care and Control.

### PPMTalk Webinar Series
Educational webinars focusing on animal care and welfare topics to increase public awareness about responsible pet ownership.

**Impact**: 8 webinars conducted to promote animal welfare education.

### Foster Care and Adoption Program
Providing temporary foster care through volunteers and screening applicants for permanent adoption.

**Impact**: 32 animals rescued, fostered, and adopted into responsible homes.
