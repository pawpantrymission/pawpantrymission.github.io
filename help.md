<section class="container">
  <h2 class="section-title">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
  <div style="max-width: 800px; margin: 0 auto; text-align: left;">
    <div class="card">
      <h3 style="color: #ff8c00;">Do I need to prove financial hardship?</h3>
      <p>No. We operate on an honor system and trust that people asking for help genuinely need it. Our goal is to make help as accessible as possible.</p>
    </div>
    
    <div class="card">
      <h3 style="color: #ff8c00;">How often can I receive help?</h3>
      <p>We understand that financial challenges can be ongoing. We'll work with you to determine a support schedule that meets your pet's needs while ensuring we can help as many families as possible.</p>
    </div>
    
    <div class="card">
      <h3 style="color: #ff8c00;">What information do you need from me?</h3>
      <p>Just basic information about your pets (type, size, any special dietary needs) and contact information. All information is kept confidential.</p>
    </div>
    
    <div class="card">
      <h3 style="color: #ff8c00;">Can you help with veterinary bills?</h3>
      <p>While we don't directly pay veterinary bills, we can connect you with low-cost veterinary clinics, emergency assistance programs, and other resources in the community.</p>
    </div>
    
    <div class="card">
      <h3 style="color: #ff8c00;">What if I have multiple pets?</h3>
      <p>We're happy to help families with multiple pets. We'll ensure you receive enough food and supplies for all your furry family members.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
