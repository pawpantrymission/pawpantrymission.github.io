# How Can I Help?

There are many ways you can support our mission to create a more compassionate community for animals in Cebu. Every contribution, no matter how small, makes a real difference in the lives of stray and community animals.

## 💖 Make a Donation

Your financial support helps us continue our vital work in animal welfare, from funding spay/neuter surgeries to providing food for community cats.

**[Donate Now](https://forms.gle/T84Cy29dgFjfN3vL6)**

### What Your Donation Supports:
- Spay/neuter surgeries for community cats
- Veterinary care for sick and injured animals
- Daily feeding of 100+ community cats
- Rescue and rehabilitation operations
- Educational programs and community outreach

*As a SEC-registered non-profit organization, your donations help us maintain our programs and expand our reach.*

---

## 🏠 Adopt a Furry Friend

Give a rescued animal a loving forever home! Our adoption process includes screening to ensure the best match between pets and families.

**[Apply to Adopt](https://forms.gle/adoption)**

**[View Available Animals](https://notion.so/adoption-available)**

### Our Adoption Process:
1. Complete our adoption application
2. Meet and greet with your potential new family member
3. Home visit (if required)
4. Adoption finalization with ongoing support

*All our rescued animals are spayed/neutered, vaccinated, and health-checked before adoption.*

## 🤝 Volunteer With Us

Join our team of dedicated volunteers and directly impact animal welfare in your community.

**[Volunteer Sign-Up](https://notion.so/volunteer)**

### Volunteer Opportunities:

**Community Cat Feeders**
- Daily feeding schedules at our managed colonies
- Monitor cat health and behavior
- Flexible scheduling available

**Foster Care Providers**
- Temporary care for rescued animals
- Socialization and basic training
- Medical care coordination

**Transportation Assistance**
- Transport animals to vet appointments
- Assist with rescue operations
- Help with adoption meet-and-greets

**Event Support**
- Community Pawntry Drive assistance
- Educational outreach events
- Fundraising activities

**Administrative Support**
- Social media management
- Documentation and record keeping
- Grant writing and fundraising

### Requirements:
- Genuine love and commitment to animal welfare
- Reliability and responsibility
- Basic animal handling knowledge (training provided)
- Own transportation preferred for some roles
