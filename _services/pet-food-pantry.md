We provide free pet food to families experiencing financial hardship, ensuring that pets receive proper nutrition while staying with their loving families.

Our pet food pantry operates on a no-questions-asked basis, providing dignified assistance to community members who need temporary support. We stock high-quality dry and wet food for dogs and cats of all sizes, including special dietary options when available.

## What We Provide

- High-quality dry dog and cat food
- Wet/canned food when available  
- Special diet foods for pets with health needs
- Puppy and kitten nutrition
- Senior pet food options
- Treats and supplements

## How It Works

1. **Contact Us** - Reach out via phone, email, or social media
2. **Brief Conversation** - We'll discuss your pet's needs (size, dietary requirements)
3. **Pick Up or Delivery** - Collect food at our distribution events or arrange delivery
4. **Ongoing Support** - Return as needed, no time limits

We believe that pets are family members, and temporary financial difficulties should never force a family to surrender their beloved companion. Our pantry ensures that lack of food is never a barrier to keeping pets and families together.
