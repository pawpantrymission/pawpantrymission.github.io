Join our team of dedicated volunteers who help make our mission possible. There are many ways to get involved, from food distribution events to administrative support.

Our volunteers are the heart of Paw Pantry Mission. Without their dedication and compassion, we couldn't serve as many families and pets in need. Whether you have an hour a month or several hours a week, there's a volunteer opportunity that fits your schedule and interests.

## Volunteer Opportunities

### Food Distribution Events
- **Setup and organization** of distribution sites
- **Client assistance** and food selection guidance
- **Loading and unloading** donations and supplies
- **Crowd management** and flow direction
- **Cleanup and site restoration**

### Administrative Support
- **Data entry** and record keeping
- **Social media** management and content creation
- **Phone calls** and client communication
- **Grant writing** and fundraising support
- **Event planning** and coordination

### Community Outreach
- **Resource fair** participation and setup
- **Presentation** development and delivery
- **Partnership building** with local organizations
- **Educational material** distribution
- **Awareness campaigns** in the community

### Special Skills Needed
- **Graphic design** for promotional materials
- **Photography** for events and social media
- **Translation services** for non-English speakers
- **Professional services** (legal, accounting, marketing)
- **Technical support** for website and systems

## Volunteer Requirements

- **Background check** for certain positions
- **Orientation session** to learn about our mission
- **Reliability** and commitment to scheduled times
- **Compassionate attitude** toward people and animals
- **Physical ability** for some positions (lifting, standing)

## Getting Started

### Application Process
1. **Contact us** to express interest in volunteering
2. **Complete** volunteer application and background check
3. **Attend** orientation session
4. **Choose** volunteer opportunities that match your interests
5. **Begin** making a difference in your community

### Time Commitments
- **One-time events** for trying out volunteering
- **Monthly commitments** for regular supporters
- **Weekly involvement** for dedicated volunteers
- **Project-based** work for specific skills
- **Seasonal helping** during peak times

Volunteering with Paw Pantry Mission is rewarding work that directly impacts families and pets in your community. Join us in ensuring that no pet goes hungry and no family has to choose between keeping their pet and meeting other basic needs.
