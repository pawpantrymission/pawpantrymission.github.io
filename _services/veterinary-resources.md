Connecting families with affordable veterinary care, vaccination clinics, and emergency medical assistance for their pets when budget is a concern.

We understand that veterinary care can be expensive, but pets deserve medical attention when they need it. Our resource connection service helps families find affordable options for their pet's healthcare needs.

## Low-Cost Veterinary Options

- **Community clinics** offering reduced-rate services
- **Mobile vaccination** clinics and wellness events
- **Spay/neuter programs** with sliding scale fees
- **Emergency assistance** funds for critical care
- **Specialized services** for specific health needs

## Types of Care We Help With

### Preventive Care
- Annual wellness exams
- Vaccinations and boosters
- Parasite prevention
- Dental cleanings

### Emergency Services
- Urgent care situations
- Emergency surgery assistance
- Critical medication needs
- After-hours care options

### Specialty Services
- Spay/neuter services
- Microchipping
- Senior pet care
- Chronic condition management

## How We Help

We maintain relationships with local veterinarians, clinics, and animal welfare organizations to provide you with:

- **Referrals** to low-cost providers in your area
- **Financial assistance** information and applications
- **Payment plan** options and arrangements
- **Emergency fund** connections for critical care

## Getting Started

Contact us to discuss your pet's medical needs. We'll help you find appropriate, affordable care options and may be able to assist with costs through our network of partners and emergency assistance programs.

Remember: we'd rather help you afford veterinary care than see you surrender your pet due to medical expenses.
