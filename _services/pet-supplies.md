Beyond food, we provide essential pet supplies including leashes, collars, toys, bedding, and grooming supplies to help families care for their pets properly.

Caring for a pet involves more than just food - they need proper equipment, comfort items, and basic supplies to stay healthy and happy. Our supply assistance program helps families obtain necessary items for their pets' wellbeing.

## What We Provide

### Safety & Control
- Leashes and collars in various sizes
- Harnesses for better control
- ID tags for pet identification
- Car safety restraints

### Comfort & Enrichment
- Pet beds and blankets
- Toys for mental stimulation
- Scratch posts for cats
- Chew toys for dogs

### Health & Grooming
- Basic grooming supplies
- Nail clippers and files
- Pet shampoo and conditioners
- Dental care items

### Feeding Supplies
- Food and water bowls
- Elevated feeders for large dogs
- Travel food containers
- Treat storage containers

## How Supply Assistance Works

Supplies are provided based on availability and your pet's specific needs. We receive donations of new and gently used items from community members and pet supply drives.

### Request Process
1. **Contact us** with your specific supply needs
2. **Tell us about your pet** (size, age, special requirements)
3. **Check availability** of requested items
4. **Pick up or delivery** arranged based on your schedule

## Special Programs

- **New Pet Startup Kits** for families adopting pets
- **Senior Pet Comfort** packages for older animals
- **Puppy/Kitten Essentials** for young pets
- **Emergency Supply** assistance for sudden needs

All supplies are provided free of charge to families in need. We believe that having the right supplies helps pets stay healthy and strengthens the bond between pets and their families.
